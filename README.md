# StarChart

StarChart helps comic readers find their next story and know what to read next.

## What it is

Most comic sites list issues. StarChart is built around the stories inside them. You describe what you are in the mood for, and it finds stories that match, even if you do not know the title or the characters.

## What you will be able to do

- **Search by story.** Describe a plot, a mood or an idea and get matching stories.
- **Browse a publisher's history.** See a publisher's comics on a timeline.
- **See what is coming out.** A release calendar for new issues.
- **Filter your way.** Narrow things down by publisher, dates and more.
- **Explore creators and characters.** Each one gets its own page showing where they appear.
- **Find the reading order.** See which issues to read, and in what order.
- **Follow what you like.** Keep up with the publishers, series and creators you care about.
- **Know where to get it.** Find out where you can buy or read an issue.
- **Family-friendly label.** Each story is marked as suitable for families, not suitable, or unknown.

Later, StarChart will add reading lists and recommendations. Other kinds of media, beyond comics, may follow.

## Which publishers

Thirteen US publishers to start: DC, Marvel, Image, Dark Horse, Archie, Boom!, Dynamite, Titan, Oni, Valiant, Mad Cave, Vault and Ignition Press.

## Where the information comes from

The comic information comes from the [Grand Comics Database](https://www.comics.org/), a community-built archive of comics. It is cleaned up and prepared before it is used here.

## Status

StarChart is in early development. The data preparation is working. The website itself is still being built.
